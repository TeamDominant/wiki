## Your account

You manage the subscription in your account. It is available in two places with the same data:

- the Telegram bot [@teamdominant_get_bot](https://t.me/teamdominant_get_bot);
- the website [infra.dominants.link](https://infra.dominants.link) — sign in with Telegram or a linked email.

To sign in on the website with email, link it in your account first. Only addresses at popular mail
services (Gmail, Rambler and similar) are accepted — this is a requirement of our mail provider.
Addresses on custom domains will not work.

## Where to get the subscription link

On the main page of your account, press **Install**. There you will find a **Copy link** button and a
button that adds the subscription to the chosen app for you.

If you paid for a friend's subscription, they just need to open the account from their own Telegram
or email — the subscription is on the main page.

## Device limit

Plans differ only in how many devices can be connected at the same time:

| Plan | Devices |
| --- | --- |
| Start | 3–5 |
| Plus | 6–9 |
| Maximum | 10–15, more available to buy |

The network, speed and locations are the same on every plan. The number of connected devices is shown
on the main page of your account; prices are on [dominants.link](https://dominants.link/#pricing).

<Aside type="note" title="What counts as a device">
  Every **app** on every device. If one phone has, say, Koala Clash and Happ with your subscription,
  that is already two devices.
</Aside>

If the app says **“Device limit exceeded”**, no servers show up, or the connect button switches off
right away:

<Steps>

1. Open your account and tap the number of devices on the main page.

2. Remove the devices you no longer use.

3. In old apps, delete the profile with your subscription — otherwise the device comes back the next
   time that app updates the profile.

4. On the new device, [update the profile](/faq/troubleshooting/#1-update-your-profile).

</Steps>

If you need more devices, buy extra ones or switch to a bigger plan — see
[Payments](/faq/payments/#extra-devices).

## Revoke or delete a subscription

On the main page of your account, tap the subscription at the top — there are two different buttons:

- **Revoke** issues a new link and the old one stops working. Your remaining days are kept. After
  that, add the subscription to all your apps again.
- **Delete** removes the subscription completely. To delete your main subscription, first make
  another one the main.

<Aside type="caution">
  If you only need to cut off someone else's device, press **Revoke**, not **Delete**.
</Aside>

### A stranger's device appeared in the list

This happens when you sold or gave away a phone or computer without deleting the subscription from
it: the app keeps updating the profile and takes a slot again. Removing the device from the list will
not help — revoke the subscription and add the new link to your own devices.

Before worrying, check it is not your own device: after updates, Windows 11 is sometimes detected as
Windows 10 and shows up as a new device.

## Linked profile (LTE / WL)

After purchase, the main page of your account shows a second, linked subscription next to the main
one (its plan name contains LTE or WL). It uses servers that are easier to reach from mobile networks.

- Use it when the main subscription does not work, or works poorly, on mobile internet.
- Traffic on the linked profile is limited, so use the main one at home on Wi-Fi.
- Add the linked profile to the app just like the main one and switch between them in the profile list.

<Aside type="note">
  The linked profile improves reachability but cannot guarantee it under every restriction a mobile
  operator may apply — it depends on the region and the operator.
</Aside>

## Several subscriptions

If you ended up with two subscriptions (for example, you bought a new one instead of renewing),
message [support](/faq/support/) — we can merge them into one or make the right one the main.
