## Trial

The first day is free: 10 GB of traffic and 1 device. When the day or the traffic runs out, the VPN
stops connecting — that is expected, not an error. Then Start at the regular price.

To pay for a subscription during the trial, press **Change plan** in your account.

## Terms and prices

You can pay for 1, 3 or 12 months. The longer the term, the lower the monthly price — up to −25% on
the annual plan. The features stay the same. Current prices are on
[dominants.link](https://dominants.link/#pricing).

Traffic is unlimited on every plan. It is limited only during the trial and on the
[linked profile](/faq/subscription/#linked-profile-lte--wl).

## Renewal and changing plans

- **Renewal** extends your current plan.
- To move to another plan, press **Change plan**. Your remaining days do not burn: they are either
  kept or recalculated to the price of the new plan — the account shows the result before you pay.

After paying, [update the profile](/faq/troubleshooting/#1-update-your-profile) in the app. The app
only picks up the expiry date when the profile updates, so until then it may show the old date or
“0 days”. The actual date is always in your account.

## Extra devices

If your plan does not have enough devices, buy extra ones in the main menu of your account (the
device count slider) or switch to a plan with a bigger limit.

- If the device count did not change after paying, reload the account page. Still the same — message
  [support](/faq/support/) and attach the receipt.
- If you accidentally lowered the number of paid devices and cannot raise it back, message support
  as well — we will fix it.

## Auto-renewal

**To turn it off:** your account → **Payments** tab. It shows the linked payment method and the
auto-renewal switch. In the bot, it is the third tab → **Payments**.

**To turn it on:** at your next payment, leave the “save payment method” checkbox ticked.

<Aside type="note">
  If **Payments** shows no linked card, nothing is charged automatically — even if the auto-renewal
  switch is inactive or the bot sent a renewal reminder. If money was ever taken without your
  consent, message support and we will refund it.
</Aside>

## I paid, but the subscription was not extended

<Steps>

1. Check the expiry date in your account — most often the payment has already gone through.

2. Update the profile in the app.

3. If the date in the account did not change, message [support](/faq/support/) with the receipt
   or a screenshot of the payment.

</Steps>

## Refunds

Refund terms are described in the “Payment and refunds” section of the
[user agreement](/faq/terms/). In short, a refund is possible if access was not provided or the
service was not delivered due to a technical fault on our side. Contact [support](/faq/support/)
within 24 hours of the payment — each case is reviewed individually. Do not file a chargeback with your bank without messaging us first.
