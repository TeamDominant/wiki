Support works in a chat right inside your account — real people answer.

## How to write to us

<Steps>

1. Open your account on the website [infra.dominants.link](https://infra.dominants.link/). In
   Telegram, the same account opens through the [@teamdominant_get_bot](https://t.me/teamdominant_get_bot) bot.

2. Go to the support section and describe the problem. Your request becomes a ticket, and all further
   messages in the chat are added to it.

</Steps>

<LinkButton href="https://infra.dominants.link/" icon="external">Open your account</LinkButton>

If you cannot get into your account, email [support@dominants.link](mailto:support@dominants.link).

<Aside type="note">
  The old support bot is no longer used — all requests go through your account.
</Aside>

## When you will get an answer

You can write at any time. The main working hours are 08:00 to 20:00 Moscow time: if your request
arrives later, you get an automatic confirmation and the reply comes during working hours — often
sooner.

If a ticket was closed but the question remains, just open a new one.

## What to include

The more details in the first message, the faster the fix:

- **device and system** — for example, iPhone 15, Android, Windows 11;
- **app and its version** — Koala Clash, FlClashX, Rabbit Hole;
- **what exactly does not work** — the whole VPN or a specific website, game or app;
- **the server** with the problem, and whether you tried others;
- **your connection** — Wi-Fi or mobile, the provider or operator;
- **a screenshot of the error** and, if possible, logs.

How to collect logs — see [Koala Clash](/faq/koala-clash/#tabs-worth-knowing) and
[Rabbit Hole](/faq/rabbit-hole/#logs). If a file or a video will not attach, upload it to cloud
storage and send the link.

<Aside type="tip">
  Before writing, go through the [Something is not working](/faq/troubleshooting/) checklist — a
  profile update is often enough.
</Aside>

## No access to your account

If your account does not open while the VPN is on, the protection system may have blocked your IP
address by mistake. Turn the VPN off and open the account again. If that does not help, email
[support@dominants.link](mailto:support@dominants.link) and include your IP address from
[2ip.ru](https://2ip.ru) (open it with the VPN off).

## Complex setups

For routers, servers and other non-standard cases we can help over remote access (RustDesk or
AnyDesk) — we will arrange it in the ticket.

## News and outages

We post about maintenance and server outages in the [alerts channel](/faq/alerts/) — check it if
something stopped working for everyone.
