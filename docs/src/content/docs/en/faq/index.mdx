## Quick answers

**Where do I get my subscription link?**
In your account (bot or website), press **Install**. There is a button that adds the subscription
to the app for you and a **Copy link** button. More in [Installation](/en/faq/install/).

**Which app should I use?**
We recommend **Koala Clash** on every platform, plus **Rabbit Hole** on iPhone and **FlClashX** on
Android and Windows. Happ, NekoBox and Throne are not supported. Full list in
[Installation](/en/faq/install/#which-app-to-use).

**It says “Device limit exceeded”.**
Every app on every device counts as a separate device. Remove the ones you no longer use in your
account. More in [Subscription & devices](/en/faq/subscription/#device-limit).

**YouTube, Telegram or some website does not work.**
Update the profile, switch the server and go through the checklist in
[Something is not working](/en/faq/troubleshooting/).

**Mobile internet does not work.**
Try the linked profile (LTE / WL) — it is made for networks where the main subscription struggles.
More in [Subscription & devices](/en/faq/subscription/#linked-profile-lte--wl).

**How do I turn off auto-renewal?**
Your account → **Payments**. More in [Payments](/en/faq/payments/#auto-renewal).

**How do I send a website or a game through the VPN?**
On a computer, with rules in Koala Clash or FlClashX — see [Routing rules](/en/faq/routing-rules/).
